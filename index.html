About Me

I am a teacher candidate pursuing a Master of Teaching from OISE at UofT. I did my undergrad here in physics and astronomy where I grew a passion for physics education and outreach.

Working under the supervision of Lydia Cao, my research looks into the current state of teacher AI literacy and the available supports in the way of professional development and AI literacy frameworks. My research interests include AI literacy, integrated ethics in computing education, and open source data and computing. I plan to pursue doctoral studies in digital technologies in education.

Outside my studies, I enjoy electronics, programming, and computers: working on the odd webdev project which I usually attach to this site. I am also a musician, performing in my community and current Co-Director of Musical Minds Community Outreach.
