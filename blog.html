The Moral Machine

By Daniel Chui | Updated Oct 4, 2026

An overview of an activity designed for my MT2 practioner research study.

Introduction

The moral machine is a classic thought experiment from the field of artificial intelligence and automation. It sheds light on how human decision making and values are inseparable from autonomous technologies. In pairs, you will be designing an autonomous vehicle algorithm that will make a critical decision: save the passengers lives or the pedestrians.

This project has potentially distressing themes with the effect of stimulating ethical and moral dialogue around algorithmic bias, transperancy, and justice. Teachers will need to be prepared to discuss and support ethical and moral conversations among students.
