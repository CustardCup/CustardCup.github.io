Introduction

The moral machine is a classic thought experiment from the field of artificial intelligence and automation. It sheds light on how human decision making and values are inseparable from autonomous technologies. In pairs, you will be designing an algorithm that will make a simple decision: crash the car and kill the passengers or swerve and kill the pedestrians.
